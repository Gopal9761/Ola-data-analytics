I just finished an end-to-end data analytics project on 40,539 Ola ride bookings from Bengaluru.

What I did:
→ Cleaned the raw export in Python (removed 62K empty rows, fixed nulls and labels)
→ Answered 10 business questions in SQL
→ Built an interactive 5-view dashboard: Overall, Vehicle Type, Revenue, Cancellation, Ratings

What the data showed:
• Only 62.2% of bookings were completed
• Drivers cancel more than customers (17.8% vs 10.1%), and the top driver reason is personal or car-related issues
• Cash is still 55% of revenue, UPI is 41%
• Auto rides average 10 km, every other vehicle about 25 km
• Customer and driver ratings are almost uncorrelated
